# Agriprice-Dashboard
Agriculture plays a crucial role in the Indian economy, and understanding pricing dynamics of agricultural produce is essential for informed decision-making by farmers, traders, and policymakers. 
Modal prices (the most frequently quoted price for a commodity) serve as a key indicator of market behavior. Analyzing these prices across time, regions, and commodities provides insights into market trends, demand-supply relationships, and possible anomalies in pricing.

This dashboard was built using pivot tables and visualizations to explore and interpret agricultural pricing data collected from various districts and markets over multiple years. The goal is to make this data interactive and insightful for stakeholders.
